Library Moves each .pkg into ps4/<title ID>/ on its drive, so the console's installer does not pick it up. Off: pkgs stay where they are, with their names. Pkgs sent to the console's storage are added from there, with no install to wait for. They still take internal space.

On the console's drive
Restore original files Takes games off the console and puts their pkgs (game, update and add-ons) back where they were, with the names they had. Nothing is deleted. The open game is closed first.

Restore the files of chosen games

Each chosen game gets its pkgs back where they were, with its update and all its add-ons. The open game is closed first.
